A screen, not a forecast: it lists the stocks best positioned for a move by the usual leadership rules, and says why. The rules have not been backtested. ATR% is the stock's typical daily range — the realistic size of a move, and of the risk. Click a heading to sort, a tag to filter.
Data through . Universe: liquid US common stocks (price ≥ $3, $5M+ daily dollar volume) from a TradingView scan, 15 minutes delayed. RS Rtg ranks 2×3M + 6M + 12M return against that universe (99 = stronger than 99%). Grp RS is the stock's industry's RS Rating. Ext = ATRs from the 20-day.
